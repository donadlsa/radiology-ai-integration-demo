Root Cause

Customer changed HL7 sending facility code (MSH-4) during scheduled PACS maintenance without notifying the integration team. The interface engine performs exact-match routing on MSH-4 and rejected all messages from the unrecognized facility identifier CHARITE_NEW. No alias or fuzzy-match mechanism existed in the routing configuration.

Impact

67 radiology orders were not processed during the 28-minute outage window. AI analysis was suspended for Charité Berlin, affecting approximately 15 studies per hour. A 12-study backlog accumulated and was cleared within 30 minutes of service restoration. No patient safety events were reported — all orders were eventually processed.

What Went Well

  • Automated alerting detected the issue within 2 minutes of the first rejected message, well within our 5-minute SLO for detection
  • EMEA on-call engineer acknowledged the alert within 2 minutes — demonstrating strong on-call discipline and monitoring hygiene
  • Root cause identified in 15 minutes using HL7 diagnostic tooling (message trace + MSH field comparison against config registry)
  • Emergency change process was smooth — CAB approval obtained in under 5 minutes for the expedited routing table update
  • Customer communication was proactive and timely, with interim updates at 07:20 and 07:50 before formal resolution notice

What Needs Improvement

  • No proactive notification from customer about planned PACS maintenance — change impacted integration without warning
  • Interface engine should accept facility code aliases, not just exact match — single character change caused full interface failure
  • Need automated backlog processing when interface is restored — manual replay of 12 studies took an additional 27 minutes
  • On-call runbook did not include MSH-4 facility code alias check — engineer had to rely on tribal knowledge to identify the issue
