Investment Recommendations

Prioritized by impact on service reliability and team scalability

Integration Config Automation

High
Three of our top recurring incidents stem from manual integration configuration. Investing in configurable templates (facility code aliases, SR description templates, token refresh automation) would eliminate an estimated 12 tickets/month and prevent 2 P1 incidents/quarter.
  • Effort: 10 sprint points
  • ROI: 15 engineering hours/month saved, 2 fewer P1s/quarter
Status: 2 of 3 fixes approved for upcoming sprint

Proactive Monitoring Expansion

High
Current monitoring is reactive — we detect issues after customer impact. Adding synthetic transaction monitoring (test HL7 messages, DICOM studies, FHIR submissions every 15 minutes) would shift detection from reactive to proactive.
  • Effort: 8 sprint points
  • ROI: 40% reduction in time-to-detect, improved customer trust
Status: Proposal stage — needs Engineering capacity allocation

Customer Self-Service Portal Expansion

Medium
Self-service portal launched in March is deflecting 26% of tickets. Expanding to include automated DICOM routing validation and FHIR connection testing could increase deflection to 40%+.
  • Effort: 5 sprint points
  • ROI: Additional 14% ticket deflection, ~20 fewer tickets/month
Status: Roadmap Q3 2026
